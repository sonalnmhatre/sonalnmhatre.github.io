03 · PRINT & CORPORATE COMMUNICATION

Consistent communication across physical formats.

2026 desk calendar

I designed this desk calendar, which was produced and distributed to company clients and managers. The cover and month layouts share a restrained colour palette, consistent typography and a repeatable date-grid structure.

The calendar’s source illustrations were AI-generated; my contribution was the calendar design and layout.

Diwali greeting & gifting materials

A coordinated set of greeting and gift materials designed for company distribution. Festive motifs, company branding and a warm visual tone connect the card, envelope and butter-paper format.

Personalised Women’s Day card

I designed personalised cards shared with women employees at the organisation’s head office. The composition combines a celebratory typographic motif with an individual message, as internal employee communication.
